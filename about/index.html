What is Sttar?

Sttar: Social Archive is a free photo-sharing app for iPhone and Android. You post up to five photos a day with your people (friends, your school, your Greek chapter), collect a stamp for every city you visit, and turn trips into albums. Day by day, it becomes the archive of your life.

Download on the App Store · Get it on Google Play

How Sttar works

Up to five photos a day

Sttar is built for everyday moments, not polished posts. You can share up to five photos a day, each with an optional caption, location, and the friends you were with. There is no set time to post and no pressure to write the perfect caption.

A calendar that becomes your archive

Every photo is saved to a personal calendar, and each day gets a cover. Weeks become months, months become semesters, and your calendar fills in as a mosaic of your life. Posting regularly builds a streak.

City stamps

Every city has a stamp you earn by being there. Stamps fill a passport on your profile and can be attached to calendar days and albums, so you can see everywhere you've been.

Trip albums

Each trip can become its own album with your photos, the stamps you collected, and a map of the places you visited.

Your people, first

Your friends' photos show up newest first. No algorithm decides who you see. Sttar is made for your friend group and campus circle, not for strangers or follower counts.

Who Sttar is for

  • Friend groups who want a private, low-effort way to share what they did today.
  • College students: verify your school email to join your school's page and campus feed.
  • Greek life chapters: a shared space for formals, socials, and chapter events.
  • Travelers who want to remember every city and trip without keeping a separate travel journal.

Privacy

You decide what's shared. Any day or trip can be public or locked to private. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Frequently asked questions

What is Sttar?

People post up to five photos a day to a personal calendar, share them with their friends, school, and Greek chapter, collect a stamp for every city they visit, and turn trips into albums. Is Sttar free?

Yes. Sttar is free to download and use on the App Store and Google Play.

How many photos can I post per day on Sttar?

Up to five photos a day. Each post can include a caption, a location, and the friends you were with, and each day gets a cover on your calendar.

How is Sttar different from Instagram or BeReal?

Sttar is built for close groups rather than followers. Your friends' photos appear newest first with no algorithm deciding who you see, you can post up to five photos at any time of day instead of one timed photo, and every day is saved to a calendar archive along with city stamps and trip albums.

What are Sttar stamps?

Every city has a stamp that you earn by being there. Stamps fill a passport on your profile and can be attached to calendar days and trip albums.

Can I use Sttar with my university or Greek chapter?

Yes. Students can verify a school email to join their school's page and campus feed, and Greek chapters get a shared space for formals, socials, and other events.

Who can see my photos on Sttar?

You choose. Any day or trip can be public or locked to private, and Sttar is designed around sharing with your own people rather than strangers.
